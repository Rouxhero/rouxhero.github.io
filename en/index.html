Collaboration with Domain-Specific Languages for Systems Design: Modeling, Analysis and Integration

The heterogeneity of Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) tools and the management of different model versions are major challenges in ensuring the overall consistency of a system.

This thesis aims to leverage SysML v2 to develop an ecosystem that fosters collaboration, consistency and traceability between models, while identifying the potential limitations of the standard.

The new features of SysML v2, such as versioning and branching, extension mechanisms and the standard API, make model comparison and merging (diff/merge) a prime use case for experimenting with and evaluating these mechanisms, notably through the use of consistency links.
